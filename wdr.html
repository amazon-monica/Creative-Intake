Video Enhanced Custom · SLA & Copy Guidelines · SLA 与文案规范
🎬 Video Enhanced Custom — Endemic & non-endemic. Bring your own brand video and key art. CS adapts your video to WDR format with start/end slates. Best for brands with existing video assets who want premium homepage video placement.
Endemic 和 Non-endemic 均可。使用广告主自有品牌视频和素材，CS 团队适配为 WDR 格式并添加片头/片尾。适合已有视频素材、希望在首页投放视频广告的品牌。
ℹ️ Video Asset Guidelines: Start/end slates are fully produced by Amazon Creative Services. Advertisers provide the video asset and original source imagery — Amazon CS will adapt them into the slate format. Video duration: 4–8 seconds max. If minor trimming is needed, CS will handle it.
ℹ️ 视频素材说明：片头/片尾画面由 Amazon Creative Services 团队完成制作。广告主需提供视频素材和原始源图，CS 团队将其适配为片头/片尾格式。视频时长：最长 4–8 秒。如需轻微剪辑，CS 团队会代为处理。
