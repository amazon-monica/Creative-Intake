📝 Headline & Sub-headline Guidelines · 标题与副标题规范
Rule · 规则Guideline · 说明
Action-Oriented
行动导向		At least one line must use a true action verb — Shop, Explore, Discover, Experience, Try.
至少一行须使用明确的行动动词，如 Shop、Explore、Discover、Experience、Try。
Separate Statements
独立语句		Headline and sub-headline must be distinct statements. Each stands alone — do not split one phrase across them or the logo.
标题和副标题须各自成句，不可将一句话拆开或被 Logo 隔断。
Capitalization
大小写		Sentence case. Title case only for category/browse names, product titles and proper nouns.
句首大写；仅品类/浏览节点名、商品标题和专有名词可用标题式大写。
Formatting
格式		Text size is fixed and cannot be adjusted. Character widths vary — preview on a 320 px screen.
字号固定不可调整；字符宽度不一，请按 320 px 屏幕预览。
Character Limits
字符上限		Headline 16 / line, max 2 lines · Sub-headline 23 / line, max 2 lines · Disclaimer 21, 1 line. Use one of the 4 approved layouts.
标题每行 16 字符、最多 2 行；副标题每行 23 字符、最多 2 行；免责声明 21 字符、1 行。须使用 4 种认可版式之一。
